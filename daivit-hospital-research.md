## 6. Insurance finding

The hospital's own service board states **“Mediclaim facility available.”** A third-party Adajan directory currently places Daivit in its “not in network” group for the insurers it tracks. This does not establish that Daivit has no cashless partners; it only shows a conflict that must be resolved directly. [Adajan insurance directory](https://happyinsurance.in/health-insurance/surat-gujarat/adajan/)

For the website, collect an approved list of:

- Cashless insurers and TPAs
- Reimbursement-only insurers
- PM-JAY / MA / government-scheme participation
- Required admission documents
- Pre-authorisation contact and expected processing time
